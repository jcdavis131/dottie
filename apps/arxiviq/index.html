Primitives

Choice · Score · Noul

Choice

execute | escalate | halt | other

One closed action for a pair-programmer step. The sidecar picks a label instead of drafting a paragraph you have to parse.

Score

severity

An ordered severity level for the step in front of you. A rubric, not a vibe word from a chat model.

Noul

P(safe)

The probability the next action is safe to take. A named probability — not a hedge buried in prose.

How data becomes decisions

HF → factory → stamp

01 · Gather

Gather

Public Hugging Face plus observed pair-programming traces. Honest inputs only — no synthetic champion rows.

02 · Curate

Curate

Choice / Score / Noul factory: honest HELPER rows, Nimble contrastive flips (≤8 words), then staging JSONL.

03 · Stamp

Stamp

Fine-tune, then a held-out bench. Cam stamps before anything is champion. Nothing auto-promotes.

What this is not

Not a chat model

This is a decision sidecar, not a chat train control plane. The verb is decide — calibrated probabilities, not a transcript to parse. LLMVM chat and watch-the-box-train dashboards are lab archive, not this product.

What we refuse

No silent promote

  • Synthetic rows do not train a champion.
  • Checkpoints do not auto-promote.
  • Bots do not approve merge or ship.

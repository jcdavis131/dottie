## Abstract
CA-MCP offloads execution logic to specialized MCP servers that read/write shared context memory, reducing redundancy and enabling knowledge transfer. Outperforms traditional MCP by reducing LLM calls and failure frequency. Tested on TravelPlanner and REALM-Bench with statistically significant gains.

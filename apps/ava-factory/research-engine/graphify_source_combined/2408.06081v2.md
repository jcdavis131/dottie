## Abstract
Analysis of RoPE dimensions undertraining in higher dims, critical dims shift earlier. Shows larger scaling factors than L/L_train improve long context but degrade short. Proposes dual scaling factors. For Ava: apply to model's RoPE to preserve short MMLU while extending to 1M.

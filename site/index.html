How it works

One conversation in.
Finished work out.

01

Brief Dottie

Point Dottie at the outcome in plain language. She breaks it into jobs and files them in the queue.

02

Dottie routes

The GOD orchestrator claims one job at a time and assigns it across agents, tools, and repos.

03

Agents execute

Real tools, real repos. Every job runs to a recorded result — done or failed, never silent.

04

You approve

Spend, scope changes, and destructive ops escalate to you. Fail-closed, approval-gated — no exceptions.

The harness

Small core.
Serious discipline.

Hive coordination

Jobs move through a mailbox queue — pending → claimed → done. One worker, one job at a time, with a full ledger of everything that ran.

Memory that compounds

Every run is remembered: what worked, what failed, and why. Dottie gets sharper with every job she finishes.

Autonomy with a leash

Per-job approval gates. Merges, releases, and destructive actions need your explicit word. Agent approval never counts.

Local-first, zero-deps core

Standard-library Python at the heart. Runs on your machine, on your repos, under your rules. No cloud required.
